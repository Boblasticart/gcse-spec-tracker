Spec points are written from the exam board specifications at topic level. Tap a point to mark it Not done, Needs work or Done. Answering questions in Improvement or Today's 7 updates your points automatically: after three correct answers in a row I ask whether you want to class the point as mastered or keep practising, and a miss on a Done point puts it back to Needs work. Practice papers has one exam-style PDF for every topic. Where a spec lets your school choose options (Geography), mark the topics you do not study as Done or ignore them.
